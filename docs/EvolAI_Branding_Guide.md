# EvolAI — Brand Identity & Logo Creative Brief

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Exploratory brand concept; name, domain, and trademark clearance pending  
**Language:** English / Français  
**Business model:** Open-source software, paid professional services

## 1. Brand foundation

**Name:** EvolAI  
**Pronunciation/reading:** “Evolve AI” in English; “évoluer avec l'IA” in French.  
**Category:** Open-source AI Adoption & Change Enablement Platform.  
**Primary tagline:** **Understand. Adapt. Evolve.**  
**French tagline:** **Comprendre. S’adapter. Évoluer.**  
**Alternative tagline:** Understand work. Unlock potential. / Comprendre le travail. Libérer le potentiel.

### Mission

Help people, teams, and organizations understand how they work, discover useful AI opportunities, build appropriate AI capabilities, and measure improvements over time.

### Vision

Make responsible AI adoption a practical, collaborative, evidence-based process that any organization can undertake, with open tools and human-centered expert support.

### Brand promise

**Progress at your own pace, with evidence rather than hype.** EvolAI meets individuals and teams where they are and helps them follow the right path forward.

### Origin and creative metaphor

The name combines **evolution** and **AI**. It may evoke the playful idea of multiple possible evolution paths, but no visual identity should copy or reference protected Pokémon characters, silhouettes, ears, tails, color schemes, names, or iconography. Translate the _abstract concept of branching growth_ into an original, professional identity.

## 2. Audience and positioning

### Primary audiences

- Employees completing short mobile-friendly AI literacy and workflow assessments.
- Team referents facilitating audits and coordinating follow-up.
- Consultants conducting audits, workshops, and change enablement.
- Leadership reviewing maturity, opportunities, risks, progress, and return on investment.
- Open-source contributors and self-hosting IT teams.

### Positioning statement

**EvolAI is an open-source AI adoption and change enablement platform that turns real workplace insights into actionable, measurable improvements.** Unlike standalone survey tools or generic AI dashboards, it connects adaptive maturity assessment, workflow discovery, human-reviewed opportunities, implementation tracking, and outcome measurement.

### Business model language

**Software:** Free and open source; auditable, extensible, self-hostable.  
**Services:** Paid discovery, AI audits, facilitation, training, deployment, implementation, governance advice, and progress measurement.

Avoid messaging that implies a paid license is required to use the core software. State the chosen open-source license explicitly only once it has been selected and vetted.

## 3. Brand personality

**Traits:** Clear, empowering, curious, practical, trustworthy, inclusive, optimistic without hype.  
**Tone:** Direct, accessible, concise, evidence-led, respectful of employee experience.  
**Not:** Sci-fi spectacle, robotic replacement rhetoric, exaggerated ROI guarantees, infantilizing gamification, abstract AI buzzwords.

### Messaging pillars

1. **Understand first:** Start with the problem and the real workflow, not a preconceived solution.
2. **Adapt to people:** AI literacy and questions adjust to the user's level.
3. **Improve together:** Employees, referents, managers, and auditors collaborate.
4. **Measure outcomes:** Compare baseline, adoption, quality, cost, and actual impact.
5. **Open by design:** Transparent software, community contribution, and self-hosting options.
6. **Trust throughout:** Responsible data handling, clear consent/access rules, validated AI insights.

## 4. Visual system recommendation

### Creative intent

A clean, recognizable identity that communicates _evolution, pathways, learning, and human-centered progress_ at 24px favicon size as well as in presentations, mobile interfaces, and GitHub repositories.

### Suggested palette (exploratory)

| Role           | Color          | Hex       | Rationale                      |
| -------------- | -------------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| Midnight ink   | Deep navy      | `#172B3A` | Trust, professional stability  |
| Evolution teal | Teal           | `#19A999` | Progress, clarity, helpfulness |
| Discovery blue | Blue           | `#4A7FE8` | Intelligence and exploration   |
| Warm accent    | Apricot        | `#FFB86A` | Welcoming, human energy        |
| Cloud          | Soft off-white | `#F7FAFC` | Calm, accessible backgrounds   |
| Slate          | Text gray      | `#536575` | Supporting content             |

Use a primarily navy/teal system and reserve blue/apricot for secondary accents. Verify text/icon color contrast to WCAG 2.2 AA for actual UI pairings.

### Typography

- Primary direction: **Inter** or **Manrope**, depending on license verification and brand testing.
- Fallback: system sans-serif.
- Wordmark: custom-adjusted geometric sans, not a decorative gaming font.
- Prefer legible open counters, subtle rounded corners, and clear distinction between lowercase l / uppercase I where used.

### Logo deliverables

- Horizontal lockup: icon + **EvolAI** wordmark.
- Icon-only: square/circular safe area for app icon and GitHub avatar.
- Stacked lockup for narrow formats.
- Monochrome black, monochrome white, and full-color variants.
- Light-background and dark-background options.
- SVG source, transparent PNG exports (1024px, 512px, 256px), favicon (32px), social avatar.
- Minimum-size tests at 16, 24, and 48 pixels; avoid fine details.

### Design don'ts

- No Pokémon/Eevee-derived imagery or other recognizable third-party characters.
- Avoid the generic glowing brain, humanoid robot, and stock neural-network motif.
- Avoid excessive gradients, complex shadows, and hairline strokes.
- Do not rely only on color to communicate meaning.
- Do not make the identity seem like an investment/risk-scoring service; readiness assessment is one module, not the whole product.

## 5. Five logo directions & ready-to-use AI design prompts

**Shared production instruction for all five prompts:** Make an original, flat vector-oriented logo with strong geometry and distinctive negative space, suited to open-source B2B SaaS and consulting. Show an icon, a horizontal `EvolAI` wordmark, and a standalone app icon. Use a neutral presentation background, precise clean edges, no mockups, no 3D, no photographic effects, no trademarked character references. Test visual clarity at favicon size. If text rendering is inaccurate, leave the symbol separate for later typesetting.

### Concept 1 — Branching Paths (recommended)

**Core idea:** One origin leads to several possible paths; every organization evolves differently.  
**Visual:** A minimal organic-geometric Y or three-way branching path, with small endpoint shapes and a subtle upward movement.  
**Palette:** Navy + teal, optional small apricot focal point.

**Prompt:**

> Design a premium original vector-style logo for “EvolAI”, an open-source platform for AI adoption, workforce learning, and organizational change enablement. Symbolize multiple possible paths of evolution from one shared starting point using a beautifully simple branching-path mark. Blend human growth with systems thinking; make the icon feel optimistic, credible, and precise rather than playful or corporate-generic. Use deep navy #172B3A and evolution teal #19A999, with very restrained apricot #FFB86A if needed. Include a clean geometric sans-serif EvolAI wordmark and a standalone icon. Flat two-color design, elegant negative space, memorable at 24px, white background. No characters, animals, Pokémon references, brain symbols, robots, gradients, or mockups.

### Concept 2 — Evolving E

**Core idea:** The letter E progresses from simple building blocks to a complete form.  
**Visual:** Custom E monogram built from three slightly advancing modular bars or shapes.  
**Palette:** Midnight navy and blue.

**Prompt:**

> Create an original minimal brand mark for “EvolAI”, an open-source B2B AI transformation and change enablement application. Construct a distinctive capital E monogram from three modular shapes that subtly progress from smaller to larger or shift forward, suggesting learning, increasing capability, and transformation without relying on arrows. Pair with a highly legible EvolAI wordmark. Contemporary Swiss-influenced geometry, balanced proportions, restrained palette #172B3A and #4A7FE8, flat vector look, clear silhouette at 16px, light and dark monochrome variants. Avoid generic AI symbols, 3D effects, characters, and lookalike technology logos.

### Concept 3 — Growth Loop

**Core idea:** Assess → improve → measure → learn; continuous evolution.  
**Visual:** Open circular loop formed by two or three rounded segments, with a deliberate forward opening.  
**Palette:** Navy and teal.

**Prompt:**

> Design a clean, original visual identity for “EvolAI”, an open-source AI adoption and continuous improvement platform. Create a unique open-loop symbol, made of two or three precisely shaped connected segments, representing assessment, action, measurement, and learning. It should imply momentum and positive progress without using standard refresh-arrow icons. Minimal flat vector mark, smooth but not childish curves, deep navy #172B3A and teal #19A999. Include EvolAI wordmark, standalone app icon, monochrome options. Make it appropriate for enterprise audits, employee surveys, developer repositories, and mobile web. No robots, brains, infinity clichés, mascots, gradients, or mockups.

### Concept 4 — Human + System

**Core idea:** People are at the center of the transformation.  
**Visual:** Three simple connected nodes arranged as a human-centered collaboration symbol (not molecular/neuronal).  
**Palette:** Navy, blue, warm accent.

**Prompt:**

> Create a sophisticated flat vector logo for “EvolAI”, an open-source platform helping employees, teams, and organizations adopt AI responsibly. Design a compact abstract symbol of collaboration: one central human-oriented circular form connected to two or three structured forms, balancing empathy and analytical systems thinking. Avoid literal stick figures, network diagrams, molecular structures, and robotic motifs; prioritize a uniquely memorable silhouette. Use #172B3A deep navy, #4A7FE8 blue, and a tiny #FFB86A warm accent. Pair with a restrained geometric EvolAI wordmark; present icon-only and horizontal lockup, crisp at 24px, plain background, no mockups.

### Concept 5 — Adaptive Steps

**Core idea:** Progress is measurable, gradual, and accessible.  
**Visual:** A small sequence of rising, rounded tiles that form a subtle upward trail.  
**Palette:** Teal/blue/navy.

**Prompt:**

> Design an original logo for “EvolAI”, open-source AI readiness, adoption, and workplace improvement software. Depict adaptive, evidence-based growth with four compact rounded modules or steps that rise in a gentle diagonal rhythm and create one cohesive symbolic mark. Feel modern, trustworthy, inclusive, and energetic, not a stock analytics bar chart. Use restrained navy #172B3A, teal #19A999 and muted blue #4A7FE8; minimal geometry, strong empty space, clean EvolAI wordmark in a contemporary humanist sans. Produce an icon-only variant and horizontal lockup, flat vector style, legible at favicon scale, no percentages, arrows, robots, or gradients.

## 6. Master prompt for a separate brand/design agent

> You are a senior brand identity designer specializing in open-source developer tools, B2B SaaS, and human-centered technology. Create five **distinct** logo concept directions for **EvolAI**. EvolAI is an open-source AI Adoption & Change Enablement platform used by employees, team referents, auditors, and leadership to assess AI literacy, understand workflows, discover AI/automation opportunities, and measure operational improvement. The software is free and open source; a consulting business sells audit, change enablement, training, implementation, and support services. The brand promise is “Understand. Adapt. Evolve.” Its inspiration is the universal idea of multiple possible evolution pathways—not any copyrighted fictional creature or visual franchise. Propose: (1) branching paths, (2) evolving E monogram, (3) continuous improvement loop, (4) human + system collaboration, and (5) adaptive steps. For each, provide a distinct icon, horizontal EvolAI wordmark, square app avatar, black/white version, recommended colors, typographic rationale, 16/24/48px legibility assessment, and one-sentence reasoning. Use strong negative space, simple vector geometry, professional restraint, accessibility, and originality. Avoid sci-fi brains, robots, generic sparkles, gradients, mockups, and any Pokémon-associated design elements. Present concepts side by side on a neutral canvas and recommend one after comparison. Do not claim trademark originality or legal clearance.

## 9. Open-source community identity

- Put `Open Source` in product documentation and repository descriptions, not as a permanent part of the logo.
- Publish a public roadmap, contribution guide, code of conduct, security disclosure process, and transparent licensing.
- Prefer exportable formats and integrations; design for self-hosting and verifiable privacy controls.
- Separate the software community identity from paid consulting service offerings without implying the latter is necessary to use the former.

## 10. Brand validation checklist

- [ ] Search EUIPO, INPI, WIPO and other relevant trademark registers for EvolAI and confusingly similar names.
- [ ] Check domain names, GitHub organization names, package namespaces and social handles.
- [ ] Have counsel assess trademark and software/service class risks before public launch.
- [ ] Shortlist 2–3 logo directions and test recognition with prospective users.
- [ ] Test wordmark readability in French and English.
- [ ] Test icon at 16/24/48px and in light/dark modes.
- [ ] Verify contrast and accessibility.
- [ ] Commission or generate final vector source files and confirm reuse rights for all design assets.
- [ ] Select a genuine open-source license based on the desired contribution and commercial model.

**Recommendation:** Start visual exploration with **Concept 1 — Branching Paths** and **Concept 2 — Evolving E**. They best connect the evolution metaphor to an easily recognizable product mark without leaning on an existing fictional character.
