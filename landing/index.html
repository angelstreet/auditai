Evidence-based AI adoption for teams that take change seriously.

EvolAI is an open-source platform for AI adoption audits and change enablement. Employees answer short, adaptive questionnaires. Auditors review AI-assisted, source-linked findings. Leaders get validated opportunities, an adoption roadmap, governance artefacts and measured outcomes.

What it does

Six core surfaces, each designed to be evidence-first, privacy-respecting and auditable.

01

Mobile assessment

Loginless secure invite, one question per screen, adaptive branching, FR/EN, about 8 minutes.

02

AI maturity model

Three axes (literacy, practice, organisational readiness) plus a task-level opportunity index; levels L0–L5 are descriptive, not certification.

03

Workflow discovery

Last 7 days of work, friction, tools, hand-offs and safety classification — surfaced as evidence, not as judgement.

04

AI-assisted synthesis

Sensitivity classification → schema-validated extraction → themes → contradictions → interview prompts. Every claim is linked to its source; human approval is mandatory.

05

Opportunity register

From employee evidence to validated, measured outcomes: detected → … → measured or rejected. No AI-generated ROI.

06

Governance & security

Tenant isolation, minimal personal data, audit trail, retention/deletion, prompt-injection resilience, launch gates.

Principles

Four rules the codebase is held to.

01

Problem-first, AI-open

AI is one option among simplification and plain automation.

02

Evidence before claims

No AI-generated counts, timings or ROI. Self-reported until verified.

03

People & data protected

No ranking, aggregation thresholds, human approval, EU hosting.

04

No lock-in

Self-hostable, portable exports, documented APIs, provider-neutral AI.

Try it

Run the full stack on your machine in under two minutes. One command, no Docker, no external services.

STEP 1

Install & build

pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm --filter @evolai/web build
STEP 2

Boot the local runner

pnpm local:up
# READY postgres=...; web=http://127.0.0.1:3101; admin=...
STEP 3

Walk the sample survey

open http://127.0.0.1:3101/about
# click "synthetic sample survey"
# answer the six questions
# view the admin page at /admin/engagements
STEP 4

End-to-end smoke

export LOCAL_WEB_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3101
export DATABASE_URL=postgres://auditai:auditai@127.0.0.1:54340/evolai_local
pnpm local:smoke   # 14 OK lines, exit 0
