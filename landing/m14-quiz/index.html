Design rules captured for M14.
  • Every public-facing result is one of three mascots (Tortoise / Fox / Owl). The raw 1–100 level is visible only to the respondent on their own profile.
  • The 5-axis breakdown (literacy / practice / readiness / opportunity / context) uses the same three mascots per axis.
  • Badges are stored in respondent_badges + team_badges (RLS, audit log on every award), keyed by respondent_pseudonym — never a real name or email.
  • The org board aggregates by dominant mascot per team; the per-axis tier percentages are the only "percentage" on screen, and they are tier-based (how many respondents hit Fox or better), not raw score averages.
  • The mascot SVGs are inline in the bundle (no external CDN) and inherit the brand palette; the system never relies on emoji for badges.
